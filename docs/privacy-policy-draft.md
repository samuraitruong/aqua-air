Aqua Air is an offline aquarium-care journal. It does not require an account. This policy explains what information is handled by the app and the publisher.

## Information stored on your device

Tank names, descriptions, dimensions and filter information, care history and notes, selected photos, care schedules and reminder settings, language preference and water pricing settings are stored in Aqua Air's local application storage. Photos are copied into that storage for display and backup. The developer does not receive these records through the app.

The released application contains no advertising, behavioral tracking, analytics service or remote account/backend service. It does not sell or send your aquarium records or photos to the developer or an analytics provider. This statement must be rechecked whenever SDKs or features change.

## Optional access and reminders

The camera is used only when you choose to take a tank photo. The system image picker grants access to pictures you choose; the app does not require access to your entire photo library. Notifications are optional local reminders scheduled on your device. They may display tank names and activities on the lock screen depending on your phone settings. Permission denial does not prevent using the journal. You can change permissions in device settings.

The app does not request microphone, contacts, location or tracking permission. Operating-system services that provide system pickers, app installation, crash diagnostics, or backup are governed by your platform provider's policies and settings.

## Backups and sharing

You can export an .aquaair backup containing records, settings and optionally photos. Aqua Air does not encrypt exported backups. A checksum checks accidental corruption and is not encryption or access control. When you choose a destination in the system share/file sheet, that destination may receive the file and process it under its own terms. Keep backups private and share only with destinations you trust.

App data may also be included in operating-system/device/cloud backups according to the device and cloud account settings. Aqua Air does not independently run a developer-hosted cloud backup service.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it or the app/device storage is removed. Use Settings → Delete all data to clear the journal and app-managed photo/recovery files. Individual records/tanks can also be removed using the app's controls. Device uninstallation and restoration behavior depend on the operating system. Exported copies, recipient copies and system/cloud backups must be deleted separately using the corresponding provider's controls; the developer cannot access or erase them remotely.
