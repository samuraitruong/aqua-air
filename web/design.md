# Aqua Air website design

The landing page is for aquarium hobbyists discovering the app. Its immediate action is to explore the product and contact the publisher about launch. The store listings do not exist yet: do not use fake store badges, dead download buttons, invented reviews or signup claims.

Preserve Aqua Air's teal #087F8C, pale #F4FAFB and navy #12394A, the system font stack and static HTML/CSS delivery. Marketing pages use a product-led split hero, authentic app-design screenshots, varied editorial feature sections and a dark closing CTA. Content pages retain their readable policy/support layout. Motion is limited to interaction feedback with reduced-motion support.

Use image assets captured from the read-only Design/screens previews. Label them as design previews, shown in Vietnamese. Do not make new fake device/browser chrome or claim those previews show unreleased functionality. User records are not marketing assets.

Keep all links relative for GitHub Pages project hosting and custom domains. Primary CTA: Explore the app. Secondary/closing CTA: email launch enquiry to samuraitruong@hotmail.com. Publisher: Truong Nguyen. Privacy and support remain accessible in navigation and footer.

Responsive targets: 320, 375, 414, 768 px plus desktop. No horizontal overflow, overlap, clipped headings or wrapped clickable button labels. Keep visible focus styles, meaningful image alt text, semantic headings and native anchors. No JavaScript, third-party fonts, trackers or form submissions.
