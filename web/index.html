Inside Aqua Air

A record for every tank.

Set a tank’s dimensions to calculate its capacity. Include a sump and canister filter, and keep your care history alongside each tank.

Record water-test results such as pH and nutrients, with custom parameters and units when needed. Log completed water changes, plan care and choose optional local reminders. The monthly water estimate shows recorded replacement water and its cost at your configured rate.

Use the app in Vietnamese, English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, French, Bengali, Portuguese, Indonesian or Urdu. Export a backup when you want a copy of your records.

Water estimates exclude top-ups, filter waste and fixed utility charges. Reminder delivery depends on your phone settings.
